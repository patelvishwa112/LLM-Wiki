# How To Never Run Out Of Writing Ideas Again

## Key takeaways

- **Ideas are amorphous; questions have shape.** Blank-page "themes" (educate / inspire / entertain) dry up after ~8–9 posts. Switch the operator question from "what should I post?" to "what does my reader actually want to know?" (or what you needed 1–2 years ago if you have no audience yet).
- **Push past the first 10 questions.** Surface asks are table stakes. Nuanced versions create differentiation — e.g. "How do I get more clients?" → "Why do warm leads go quiet after the first call, and what reopens them?"
- **Mine where readers already ask:** X, Reddit, LinkedIn replies, YouTube comments, Facebook groups — don't invent nuance from thin air.
- **Unbundle one question into many pieces:** same question × reader archetype × outcome × adjacent problem context → 5–10 posts from one insight.
- **10 Magical Ways (answer formats):** Tips, Stats, Steps, Lessons, Benefits, Reasons, Mistakes, Examples, Questions, Personal stories. One question × format matrix builds the library.
- **Authority = time on the Q→A loop** in public, longer and more consistently than the niche — not talent mystique.

## Skeptical read

- Framework is solid craft; the free-course funnel is the commercial close. Use the question matrix without needing the paid ecosystem.
- "Never run out" assumes continuous mining and research capacity — still a discipline habit, not a free lunch.
- Format matrix can produce thin spam if nuance is skipped and only surface questions are rotated.
