# Improve Your Cognitive Skills

DJ.en (@thisisdjen). Lifestyle post aimed at crypto/business grind. Not technical.

## Advice

Sleep consistency over sacrificing sleep for work; wind-down without phone. Learn languages (cites dose-dependent "brain years younger" multilingualism numbers — treat as popular science). Read anything, 30 min a couple nights, after dinner. Creatine: cites sleep-deprivation cognitive studies and mental fatigue; gym association is incidental.

Closer: travel, study from apps, train a couple times a week, distinguish disconnect vs escape.

## Skeptical read

Uncited epidemiology on multilingualism "years younger"; creatine evidence exists but is not this post. Keep as a short productivity bookmark, not a protocol.
