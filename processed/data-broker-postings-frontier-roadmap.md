# Data broker and frontier lab postings are a public roadmap

**Source:** Hamel Husain, 2026-10-20. A quote-tweet relay of Will Manidis's observation about procurement postings, quoting Brendan Foody's Mercor referral offer.

The post is a two-hop relay rather than an argument. Hamel Husain surfaces Will Manidis's claim that there is still "unbelievable alpha" in tracking two kinds of public listings: data broker procurement postings and frontier lab job postings. Manidis's framing is that these constitute "literally a public roadmap for where the frontier is at a given moment" — the demand side of training runs has to advertise before it can spend, so its postings leak capability direction ahead of announcements.

The quoted tweet underneath, from Brendan Foody (Mercor), supplies the concrete instance: Mercor will pay up to $100K for each referral of a company that licenses its data to them, has partnered with more than 500 companies, and is targeting firms with 100+ employees holding data in code, gaming, manufacturing, healthcare, or professional services. That bounty is itself a priced disclosure — Mercor is telling the market which data verticals it currently values and roughly how much a qualified introduction is worth.

No methodology, dataset, or track record is offered; the post is an operator's assertion plus a price signal. Treat the causal claim (postings predict frontier direction) as a hypothesis worth testing against your own observation of lab hiring and data-vendor RFPs, not as an established result. The mechanism is plausible — brokers cannot book revenue without supply, so their recruiting language doubles as a demand disclosure — but it is indirect by construction, and it degrades once enough people watch the signal.

## Why it matters

For anyone building or investing in AI, this is a cheap, legal, under-used alternative-data source: procurement and hiring pages reveal training priorities months before model releases, and the referral prices paid by data vendors put a rough market value on specific data verticals. The five named domains — code, gaming, manufacturing, healthcare, professional services — are a readable shortlist of where agentic and verifiable-task training data is currently being bought, which maps closely onto where agent capability is being pushed next.

## Key points

- Claim: data broker procurement postings and frontier lab postings together form a public roadmap of the frontier at any given moment.
- Mercor offers up to $100K per referral of a company that licenses its data to Mercor.
- Mercor reports partnerships with more than 500 companies.
- Referral target profile: 100+ employees with data in code, gaming, manufacturing, healthcare, or professional services.
- The post provides no evidence or methodology; the value is the directional signal and the disclosed price, not a validated predictor.
- Relay structure matters: the claim is Manidis's, the pricing detail is Foody's, and the surrounding posts are distribution channels.
- Spread is itself the signal that the heuristic is becoming crowded: five captures in the vault over one month (Sep 20 → Oct 20), with the Mercor numbers unchanged.
