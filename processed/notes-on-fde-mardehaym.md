# notes on FDE

Third LimestoneHQ essay from @mardehaym (Mark Ajzenstadt). Labs and SIs are becoming implementation companies (Microsoft ~$2.5B / 6,000 embedded AI engineers; Amazon ~$1B; Anthropic, OpenAI, Accenture, EY). The hire title is **Forward Deployed Engineer**. Claim: that title is Palantir playbook at new scale — and **rotation + A-player hunting** will fail the way consulting already fails.

## Five lessons (vendor framing)

1. **FDE is not a rare species.** Full-stack + product curiosity + “what problem?” before “what framework?”. Those people already sit in most orgs; missing piece is a **system that develops them**.
2. **Culture looks like a factory floor.** Daily 8 AM numbers, deliverables, blockers; same quality conversation three days running. Client outcomes over perks. Grind, not campus.
3. **“Hire only the best” does not scale.** Waiting for self-starting unicorns while work sits. Develop competent curious engineers into product-owner / consultant / client-fluent shippers via months of operational pressure.
4. **Context does not survive rotation.** Quarterly FDE swaps = consulting with better branding. Capital does not replace accumulated system knowledge. Frontier-company embed can install **vendor architecture as customer infrastructure**.
5. **On-site-only economics break.** Narrow pool → unsustainable rates passed to clients. Alternative claimed: long-term embed + transfer the operating system so the **client’s own people** become FDEs.

Real scarcity named: not engineers — **managers who will enforce the standard daily**.

## Why it matters

Useful checklist when reading lab “Frontier Company / no pilots” GTM: who keeps context, whose architecture remains after renewal, and whether the motion develops client people or rents scarce titles.

## Skeptical read

LimestoneHQ sales essay + discovery-call CTA. “$9 billion” is a round-up of competing commitments, not a market-size study. Factory-floor 8 AM is anecdote, not a controlled comparison with lab FDE programs. Still a sharp frame: **rotation vs accumulation**, **hire vs develop**, **help vs vendor lock-in**.
