# To FDE, or not to FDE?

@thejessezhang (DecagonAI CEO) on forward-deployed engineers in AI GTM. FDE is now the default answer (Anthropic/OpenAI Palantir-style arms; job posts up hundreds of percent) after years of being criticized as “no real product.” Economics didn’t change — **outcomes** became the sellable unit.

## Core line (Palantir / Shyam Sankar)

**“FDEs eat pain and excrete product.”** Field pain is input to platform primitives (ontology, permissions, workflows → Foundry), not permanent cost of sale. Mature Foundry: fewer custom deploys, margins into 80s, FDE→core eng, turn down “Accenture with better software” deals.

## When FDEs are necessary

New category (e.g. 2026 accounting agents): no established user journey — not even the customer knows what they want. Last mile is **discovery**, not delivery. Sit in the room; watch production break outside the test suite.

Contrast mature SaaS CRM 2015: workflows already invented.

## The trap: not starting — not stopping

Keeping FDEs is easier every sprint: avoid product tradeoffs, config boundaries, saying no. Then: cost-to-serve doesn’t fall, margins capped, growth = hiring, every field fix is a deferred product decision. Each deploy should make the next easier. Few startups have Palantir-scale 8-figure deals to subsidize forever.

## Don’t confuse titles

| Mode | Nature |
|------|--------|
| **FDE / discovery** | Unknown workflow; encode patterns into product |
| **Implementation** | Known spec (integration); increasingly product/agent-doable |

Bundling both under “FDE” launders a services org as product investment.

## Decagon choice

Customer service = high-volume, repeatable, decomposable → **product-driven** over FDE-driven. Drivers: continuous iteration speed; vendor sovereignty (keys to customer team). Escalations → requirements, not patches.

Claimed results:

- **~2/3** of deployment work autonomous via **Duet** (config, iteration, tuning tail)  
- First **AOP** launch avg **few days** (banks, airlines, telcos)

Still partner for outcomes; own the build while enabling the customer.

## Decision questions

- Bespokeness in **their** environment or **your** product gaps?  
- Last mile irreducible or unbuilt?  
- FDEs discovering or absorbing?  
- What shipped into product last time one came back?  

If they eat pain and excrete more pain → services business, not FDE team.

## Why it matters

Clean GTM/product doctrine for agent startups: FDE as **temporary discovery instrument**, product as leverage. Pairs with process-maturity and enterprise agent notes; counters “hire FDEs for everything” fashion.

## Skeptical read

CEO narrative + Decagon metrics self-reported. Category fit matters — CS may productize more cleanly than highly bespoke verticals. Still the right checklist for when to pull FDEs out.
