# Everything I Learnt in Reverse

Finlay (@finlayekins), 2026. X Article on bootstrapping Hourglass AI. Numbers are self-reported.

## Arc

Quit engineering May 2025. Month 1: $700. Annualized one $8k month as "91.2k ARR," then two months of travel dropped revenue to ~$150. Nov–Feb valley (January $0). Teamed with Batko (Startmate) → Hourglass AI. May–Aug: $55k / $40k / $105k / $72k, bootstrapped. System: paid audits converting 80–90% to builds, retainers, referrals, internal AI for admin/pricing.

## Lessons in reverse

1. Quitting is the small risk (downside is the job you already have).
2. Know the real why (leverage / not being told what to do) — "getting rich" does not survive grim months.
3. Hedonic baseline: build for the Tuesday after the win; don't annualize best month.
4. Solo business stops when you stop. No system = a job with no sick leave.
5. Grim months are the course fee; difficulty is the moat.
6. Sales cycles lag the bank balance ("yes" → invoice months later).
7. Effort compounds ~2 months before revenue.
8. Right people change what the business *is* — combo before niche.
9. Failing bought the unglamorous knowledge of where AI is value vs toy.
10. Worked 9–5 through nearly all of it; start the thing.

## Skeptical read

Anecdotal revenue curve and conversion rates. Extract the **system vs job** and **valley-as-tuition** points, not the ARR screenshots.
