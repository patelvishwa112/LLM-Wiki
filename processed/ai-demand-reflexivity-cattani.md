# Nobody is talking seriously about AI demand

Giovanni Cattani (@giovannicatt3, 2026-09-01). Analysts model infinite demand for any supply. He is bullish on **AI demand overall**, but argues **frontier-token** demand is partly **reflexive** — great on the way up, fragile on the way down. All figures in the piece are **estimates**.

Hook: Dwarkesh / $100B per GW; if Anthropic monetized expected capacity, a **$500B ARR** end-2026 hypothetical. 2028 AI capex vs France’s budget.

## Tokens as time + 2×2 labor

METR 80%-time horizon chart: tokens ≈ **delegable time** (o3 ~30 min, Mythos ~3h). Cross with **bounded vs unbounded**:

| | Short horizon | Long horizon |
|--|---------------|--------------|
| **Bounded** (complexity saturates: taxes, simple frontend) | Already one-shottable | Coming; then **cheapest** model wins. ROI = cost cut, not extra revenue. Open/non-frontier. **No reflexivity.** |
| **Unbounded** (always more: AI R&D, chips, space, software, trading alpha) | — | Power-law; spend as much time as possible. ROI = **upside**. **Frontier only** (F1 car, even if it lasts 9 months). |

## Where frontier inference $ comes from (his split)

Guess: **~50%** of frontier **inference** revenue from three unbounded loops:

1. **AI R&D** (~20%) — labs 60/40 train/infer rumor; other labs + applied post-train using frontier for research/synth data.
2. **Software** (~15%) — startups/AI-native, not F500; ship → raise → more tokens.
3. **Trading** (~15%) — power-law spenders; P&L feedback is instant.

Loop: more tokens → more revenue/capital → more tokens. Six-month closed-source lead is enough to take the market here. Bounded-task startups don’t get that flywheel (savings shared with RLaaS; ceiling).

## Double edge

Those three are **correlated and super-procyclical**. A hit to one could be 15–20% direct and **~50%** via contagion (lab equity → VC token spend → public markets → quant spend). Triggers he lists: capability-slowing regulation, higher rates, exogenous shock. Post-ChatGPT era hasn’t seen a demand-side downturn (coincident with a NASDAQ bottom). Time to **model demand**, not only supply — especially for buildout-exposed names.

## Value accrual

Bounded labor GDP → data-center stack; little to the app company. Unbounded value to teams that can **allocate capex/tokens** at long horizon. Markets today love predictable cash flow; he expects a repricing toward “several more Elons.”

## Skeptical read

- Attribution is “let’s say” arithmetic, not disclosed mix.
- $500B ARR / France-budget capex are illustrations.
- Trading-as-double-digit is rumor-conditional.
- Useful **taxonomy** even if the 20/15/15 is wrong: bounded→cheap, unbounded→frontier, reflexivity→fragility.
