# How I Find Problems to Solve as a Staff Engineer

Eray (@erayajk). Tweet thread; card points at lalitm.com (not fetched).

1. Listen to how people talk about work (meetings, email, chat) rather than asking what they need.
2. A requested fix is a symptom. Watch the workflow; talk to system owners and people who span the stack.
3. Don't patch on first hearing. Wait for overlapping requests; treat the pattern as a hypothesis.
4. Before coding: proposal or throwaway prototype. Messy problems → specialized pieces, not one perfect system.
5. Path by risk: easy/helpful just do it; uncertain → disposable code; high-stakes → review agreement first.
6. Shape problems so others lead execution. Trust earned → better next problems.
