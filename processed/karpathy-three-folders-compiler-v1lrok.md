# Karpathy's Three Folders Are a Compiler, Not a Cabinet

Valrok (@v1lrok), 2026-09-09. Quote-tweet of @rvaniaaaa's X Article *The Second Brain Is Not a Storage System. It's a Compiler.* Reads Karpathy's viral three-folder diagram (claimed 5k stars / 16M views).

## Distinction

A filing cabinet **retrieves**: notes accumulate, search when needed, understanding on day 1000 equals day 1. That's accumulation wearing memory's clothes.

A compiled wiki **compiles**: raw stays untouched; the model reads once, connects to already-compiled knowledge, writes synthesis permanently. Later questions are lookups in something that got smarter without you searching.

## CLAUDE.md as the uncredited center

Identity, current work, already-tried. Auto-read every session so the model stops being re-introduced. The folders get the screenshots; this file is why the system does not reset every morning.

Closer: almost none of the viewers built it. The ones who did stopped resetting.
