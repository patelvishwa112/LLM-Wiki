# How to clear any technical interview in 2026

@shubhranshu2009: interviews shifted more in ~2 years than prior decade. Puzzle grind still helps a bit; alone it’s nowhere near enough — AI solves most classic puzzles instantly too.

## What’s actually tested

| Old | New |
|-----|-----|
| Correct algorithm under time, no help | Clear thinking, explain reasoning, catch own mistakes, decide when path isn’t obvious |

Code matters less; **how you arrive + whether you understand** matter more. Memorize-only prep targets a disappearing test.

## Plan

1. **Patterns deep, not 500 random problems** — ~10–15 cores (two pointers, sliding window, graph, basic DP…). Why it works, problem signals, slight variants. Interviewers dodge well-known memorizable Qs.

2. **Narrate thinking out loud** (most underrated) — even alone: options considered, rejected paths, tradeoffs, edge cases mid-stream. Silent correct finish loses to spoken reasoning + preemptive edge-case catch.

3. **AI usage, honestly** — extremes fail (never / AI does all thinking). Middle: AI for boilerplate + exploration; **read, understand, explain before ship**; prove with real shipped AI-assisted code.

4. **Real stories, not scripts** — 3–4 structured memories: hard problem, why hard, decision vs alternative, outcome, **what you’d redo**. Honest tradeoffs beat perfect stories.

5. **System design for the role** — blocks (DB scale, cache, service comms, growth tradeoffs) > memorized Netflix/Twitter. Clarify before design; principle transfer over regurgitate.

6. **I don’t know** — don’t freeze/bluff. Uncertain → reason aloud how you’d figure it out (decompose, what to look up and why). Calm under uncertainty > already knowing.

7. **Company-specific research** — real product challenges and why *this* space interests you; generic “innovative” reads fake.

## Bottom line

> Prepare for the interview that’s actually happening, not the one that used to happen.

Winners: understanding, honest reasoning (incl. AI), thinking clearly out loud when the answer isn’t pre-memorized.
