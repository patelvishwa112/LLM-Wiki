# How to Become a Forward Deployed Engineer (8 steps)

## Key Takeaways

- ~95% of enterprise AI projects show no measurable ROI (MIT NANDA / large deployment studies cited) — failure is integration/ops/compliance, not demo model quality.
- **FDE** = embed weeks with customer, ship custom integrations; “founding engineer on someone else’s product.” Loop: embed → ship → core productizes.
- Titles to search: FDE, FDSE, Applied AI Engineer, Deployment/Solutions/Founding (customer-facing).
- Comp sketch (mid-2026 article): Palantir median ~$215K TC; frontier mid ~$385K, staff ~$610K, senior ~$785K–$1M+ with equity 55–70%.
- Breadth > depth: Python + TS, one cloud, one DB, one UI; product judgment + ROI language.
- Ship AI, don’t train: evals, hallucinations, retrieval, tool calls, latency/cost/reliability/security tradeoffs.
- Portfolio artifacts enterprises buy: **MCP servers**, **agent skills**, **sub-agents** against messy real systems (docstrings with *when* to use tools; audit rows for compliance).
- One real production deployment with business outcomes is non-negotiable.
- Interview killer round: **discovery** — research interview with customer, not jumping to solutions.

## Skeptical read

Comp figures and the 95% statistic are marketing-grade; still useful as a role definition and artifact checklist for applied/enterprise AI work.

## Why it matters

Clear career map adjacent to applied AI engineer guides: the scarce skill is surviving contact with legacy systems and skeptical ops, not another fine-tune tutorial.
