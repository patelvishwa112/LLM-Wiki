Each chapter folder also carries a per-topic further-reading list pointing at primary sources, e.g.
Amazon Dynamo (SOSP 2007), Google BigTable (OSDI 2006), Cassandra architecture docs, Google Maglev
load-balancer paper, Tom White's consistent-hashing write-up and Stanford CS168 notes, Uber's
`uber-go/ratelimit` implementation, Martin Fowler's CircuitBreaker bliki entry, and engineering
posts on Netflix encoding and Discord's Elixir scaling. The README states the notes are a work in
progress; a rendered version is published at pagefy.io/system-design/system-design-interview-by-alex-xu.
